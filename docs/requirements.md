# Product requirements — October 2026

Malawi Airlines Roster Builder supports schedulers, crew control, pilots and cabin crew. Shared Laravel APIs own operational data and decisions. This document preserves the prototype scope; docs/roadmap.md distinguishes shipped setup from future product work.

## Planning
Rosters run for one week or two weeks (from a Monday) or a calendar month (from the 1st), in base-local dates, and never overlap. Staff see a timeline of past and upcoming rosters and the gaps between them; rosters can start from about six months back to two years ahead, and rosters that have ended are read-only history. Creating a roster freezes the current duty rules for it. Staff can open any crew member's individual roster; crew see only their own.
The roster generator expands enabled patterns into dated trips for the roster's remaining operating days (a build with nothing left to plan says so). Each trip requires one CPT, one FO and the aircraft cabin count. The same crew fly a whole rotation, including night stops, and change only once it is back at base. Hardest seats (fewest eligible crew) are filled first, each with the legal crew member who has the lowest fairness score: share of their weekly working hours already used (%) + block hours flown this month + 1.5 × trips this month. Never automatically break a rule; leave the seat open with the rejection reasons (the build message names the most common ones). Missing crew documents warn by default; the duty rules can make them block assignment instead. Rebuilds keep manual (locked) seats and trips that have already operated.
Each crew member may have contracted weekly working hours; the generator never plans more duty than that in a Monday–Sunday week. Without them, only the rolling seven-day duty limit applies.
The scheduler can change any seat of a draft week whose trip has not operated: legal candidates first, and a recorded reason is required to accept a rule break. Inactive crew, the wrong position or two seats on one trip are never allowed. Publishing requires a build and no unaccepted rule conflict; open seats are allowed. A published week can be reopened as a draft.
Conflicts are recalculated live (leave, documents, ratings and other duties can change after a build): position, base, rating, documents, exclusions, leave/day off/untimed SIM or standby, overlap, minimum rest, seven-day duty, monthly block, consecutive days, monthly days off and weekly working hours; plus open seats, disabled flights and types with no serviceable airframe as warnings.
After the hardest-first fill the generator rebalances: automatic seats move from the crew member using the largest share of their weekly capacity to a legal crew member with less, while that narrows the gap. It then plans standby on free days with flying from the crew member's base, for crew who would exceed the maximum weekly days off (prorated for leave and part weeks), only when legal; standby starts at 06:00 LT for 8 hours (config/roster.php) and is replaced on rebuild.
Seats can be excluded for a crew member (never assigned to that trip again until allowed) and the last manual change of a seat can be undone. Assigning a flight clears standby on its days.
Outputs: CSV (staff: all seats; crew: own), PDF files (the full landscape grid or one page per crew member — individual, selected or all — with the authorised airline logo, continuation pages and page numbers), iCalendar per crew member, printable view, and roster emails with a calendar attachment and the crew control signature, sent through the queue and logged per crew member.
Reports (staff): roster coverage by week, crew hours for any period, manual overrides with reasons, crew document expiry, fleet status and maintenance due, flight pattern utilisation and the audit log, for periods up to a year, on screen and as CSV or PDF.
CSV import of crew, flight patterns and day planning with templates, header aliases, flexible dates, a preview of every row, merge or replace, and an atomic, audited commit that is refused when rows have errors or the data changed since the preview. Versioned JSON backup and restore of all operational data (administrators), never including accounts or secrets.
Self-service password reset by email or username with single-use, expiring links; the answer never reveals whether an account exists.
Hours: block and duty hours accumulate per pilot and cabin crew member from published rosters (flown once released, scheduled until then) for this week, month, last 28 days, year and last 12 months. Pilots' reports lead with block hours, cabin crew with duty hours; timed SIM and standby count as duty.
Accounts: administrators create, edit and delete every account type (administrator, scheduler, crew control, pilot, cabin crew). Schedulers create and edit pilot and cabin crew accounts only and cannot delete. Crew control has no account access. Pilots and cabin crew sign in with read-only access to their own published roster, calendar and hours. Everyone can change their own password; a password set by someone else signs that person out everywhere.
The operations dashboard shows, at a glance: seat coverage, roster conflicts for this and next week, fleet availability, flights enabled, maintenance and document alerts, the state of the last, current, next and following roster (any length), today's trips, and fleet, maintenance and document items that need attention.

## Default configurable rules
Report 60 minutes before first departure; release 30 minutes after last arrival. Maximum daily duty 13 hours; minimum rest 12 hours, including night stops; rolling seven-day duty 60 hours; calendar-month block 100 hours; maximum consecutive duty days 5; minimum monthly days off 8 (leave counts). Maximum weekly days off 4, Monday–Sunday, with prorated edge weeks and leave excluded; 7 disables this rule. Planned standby may fill workload gaps only when legal and not on protected days off.
Position, aircraft rating, base, licence, medical and recurrent expiry all constrain eligibility. Missing documents must have an explicit policy before automatic assignment.

## Fairness
Score = block hours + 1.5 × trips + 4 for late finish after 18:00 followed by start before 07:00 − 1 for adjacent duties. Show pilot/cabin block ranges, assignment rationale, next-best candidates and rejected reasons.

## Operational data
Aircraft types: unique code, cabin requirement 0–20, roster palette. Prevent deletion while referenced by flights, ratings or airframes.
Airframes: unique registration (e.g. 7Q-TBA), aircraft type, airframe hours, notes. The Fleet page offers two states, Available or Not available, with an optional reason; the API also keeps the maintenance and AOG variants of "not available". Every change is audited with its time. New airframes start available; status is never set through the general edit form. Airframes with maintenance history cannot be deleted (mark unavailable instead). Flights show a warning when every registered airframe of their type is unavailable.
Maintenance records: airframe, check type (line check, A-check, C-check, scheduled inspection, component/hard-time, servicing, other), description, performed date, optional airframe hours at completion, next due date and/or next due airframe hours, notes, recorded-by user. The latest record of each check type per airframe sets its next due point. Alerts: overdue when the base-local date has passed or airframe hours reach the due hours; due soon within 14 days or 50 hours (config/roster.php). Alerts never change aircraft status automatically.
Flight enable/disable: a flight pattern can be disabled and re-enabled without resubmitting its legs; disabled patterns stay on file and must not be planned.
Flights: unique uppercase code, aircraft, operating weekdays (Monday=0), connected legs with airports, times entered and shown in GMT (stored base-local with the rules' offset), returning to base. Each new leg starts where the previous one landed; after a leg to an outstation the editor asks whether the crew night-stop there and for how many nights. Night stops are only at outstations (a night at base ends the rotation, crew change there). Rotations can last up to ROSTER_MAX_TRIP_DAYS days (default 14). Add/edit/copy/delete; split/join stops; returns and night stops. Validate duty/rest and warn on turnarounds below 20 minutes or bases lacking rated captains.
Crew: name, optional email, CPT/FO/CC, base, aircraft ratings, weekly working hours (optional), document expiry dates. Cabin can be all-rated. Show expired, expires in period, and within 30 days afterwards.
Day planning: leave, simulator, standby and protected day off over date ranges with notes, edited from the roster grid; timed activities count as duty.
Manual assignment: replace/remove/undo; legal choices first; persist locks; flag overrides; assigning a flight clears standby atomically.

## Rosters and outputs
Whole grid and individual view, rank/base filters, duty details and flags. Summary: filled/open seats, breaks, expiry alerts, pilot/cabin ranges.
Print landscape roster or per-person pages. HTML, CSV, individual/selected/full PDFs with authorized airline logo, continuation pages, UTC ICS events. Email attachment delivery and logs, with Crew Control signature. Share-sheet fallback for mobile is a future client concern. No email is sent from demo seeders.

## Import and recovery
CSV comma/semicolon/tab, aliases for columns, flexible dates including Excel serial dates, operating-day formats, display-zone entry conversion. Preview every row before commit. Merge or replace by kind; match crew by name and flights by code with ambiguity handling. Download templates. Versioned JSON backup/restore with validation, authorization, atomic replacement and audit. Preserve compatibility through an explicit crew-roster-v2 importer, not unrestricted mass assignment.

## Demonstration data
Q400 (2 cabin), B737 (3 cabin); LLW and BLZ bases; LB1, LB2, LN1, LH1, BJ1, LJ1, LD1, LK1, LA1 (night stop). 48 fictional crew (12 CPT, 12 FO, 24 CC), example.com emails, activities and expiry examples. Four fictional airframes (7Q-DMA–7Q-DMD) across all statuses with overdue, due-soon and current maintenance examples. No default production credentials. The Malawi Airlines logo supplied in logo/ is served from public/images/malawi-airlines-logo.png; confirm usage rights before external distribution.
